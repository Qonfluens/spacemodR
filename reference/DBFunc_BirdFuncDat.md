# Bird Functional Traits Dataset (EltonTraits 1.0)

Dietary category percentages, foraging strategies, and functional traits
for birds.

## Usage

``` r
DBFunc_BirdFuncDat
```

## Format

A data frame containing species dietary breakdowns (summing to 100

- SpecID:

  Unique species identifier.

- PassNonPass:

  Indicates whether the bird is a Passerine or Non-Passerine.

- IOCOrder:

  IOC taxonomic order.

- BLFamilyLatin:

  BirdLife International family Latin name.

- BLFamilyEnglish:

  BirdLife International family English name.

- BLFamSequID:

  BirdLife International family sequence ID.

- Taxo:

  Taxonomic grouping code.

- Scientific:

  Scientific name of the bird species.

- English:

  Common English name of the bird species.

- Diet.Inv:

  Percentage of invertebrates in the diet.

- Diet.Vend:

  Percentage of vertebrate endotherms consumed.

- Diet.Vect:

  Percentage of vertebrate ectotherms consumed.

- Diet.Vfish:

  Percentage of fish consumed.

- Diet.Vunk:

  Percentage of unknown/unclassified vertebrates in the diet.

- Diet.Scav:

  Percentage of scavenging activity.

- Diet.Fruit:

  Percentage of fruits consumed.

- Diet.Nect:

  Percentage of nectar/pollen consumed.

- Diet.Seed:

  Percentage of seeds/nuts consumed.

- Diet.PlantO:

  Percentage of other plant tissues consumed.

- Diet.5Cat:

  Simplified 5-category diet classification.

- Diet.Source:

  Source of the dietary information.

- Diet.Certainty:

  Certainty score for the diet data.

- Diet.EnteredBy:

  Identifier of the person who entered the diet data.

- ForStrat.watbelowsurf:

  Percentage of foraging time spent underwater.

- ForStrat.wataroundsurf:

  Percentage of foraging time spent at the water surface.

- ForStrat.ground:

  Percentage of foraging time spent on the ground.

- ForStrat.understory:

  Percentage of foraging time spent in the understory.

- ForStrat.midhigh:

  Percentage of foraging time spent in the mid-high canopy.

- ForStrat.canopy:

  Percentage of foraging time spent in the upper canopy.

- ForStrat.aerial:

  Percentage of foraging time spent in aerial foraging.

- PelagicSpecialist:

  Indicator of pelagic specialization.

- ForStrat.Source:

  Source of the foraging strategy data.

- ForStrat.SpecLevel:

  Specificity level of the foraging strategy record.

- ForStrat.EnteredBy:

  Identifier of the person who entered the foraging data.

- Nocturnal:

  Indicator of nocturnal activity.

- BodyMass.Value:

  Body mass value in grams.

- BodyMass.Source:

  Source of the body mass information.

- BodyMass.SpecLevel:

  Specificity level of the body mass record.

- BodyMass.Comment:

  Notes on body mass data.

- Record.Comment:

  General comments about the record.

## Source

EltonTraits 1.0 database.
